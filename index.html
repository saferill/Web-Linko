FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Linko and local sharing.

Is Linko free and open-source?

Yes, Linko is 100% free and open source under Apache 2.0. There are zero advertisements, trackers, or hidden fees.

Does it use my cellular data?

No. Linko strictly communicates over your local area network (LAN/Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot). Internet bandwidth is never touched.

Is my data transfer secure?

Yes. All transmissions use peer-to-peer TLS encryption certificates directly between the two devices.

Where are received files stored?

By default, files are saved to your system's default Downloads folder. You can configure the destination in Settings.
